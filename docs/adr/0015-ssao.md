# 0015 - Screen-space ambient occlusion, depth only, half resolution

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-26
- Decides for: 4.9
- Depends on: 0011 (HDR target), 0014 (material maps)

## Where the AO comes from, and what it is not

The mesher already bakes corner AO into the vertex (`v_ao`, 0..15, task 3.12) from the
six neighbouring blocks it can see. That is exact for a corner between three blocks
and blind to everything further away: a wall meeting a floor, a block sitting under an
overhang, the inside of a doorway. Screen-space AO covers exactly that gap, and the two
multiply rather than one replacing the other.

## Decision

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Input | the depth buffer only - no G-buffer, no normals pass |
| Normal | rebuilt from the gradients of the reconstructed view-space position |
| Samples | 12, golden-angle spiral on the hemisphere, rotated per pixel by a hash of the fragment coordinate |
| Resolution | half, then a 4x4 box blur |
| Applied | in the resolve pass, `hdr *= mix(1, ao, 0.85)`, in linear light before exposure |
| Toggle | F12; cost in the once-per-second stats line as `ssao` |
| Change to post | the depth attachment became a texture instead of a renderbuffer so the pass can read it |

## The bug that cost the first attempt

`cross(up - position, right - position)` points **away** from the eye in a view space
that looks down -Z. The sample hemisphere went into the surface, nearly every sample
tested as occluded, and the frame came out evenly grey: mean absolute difference from
the no-AO frame 13.37, 54 percent of pixels changed. Reversing the cross product took
that to 3.49 and 14 percent, and the image to contact shadow in the creases with open
faces left alone.

That is worth recording because the symptom looks like "AO is too strong" and invites
turning the strength down, which would have hidden the sign error under a weaker wrong
answer.

## Measured

Same pose and sun, AO on vs off:

| | on | off |
|---|---|---|
| terrain mean luminance | 43.7 | 47.4 |
| pixels differing by more than 8 levels | 128k of 921k (14 %) | - |
| cost | **0.057 ms** at half resolution | - |
| frame | 120.0 fps, worst 8.69 ms | 120.0 fps, worst 8.93 ms |

The pass is 0.057 ms against a 2.0 ms allowance, so there is room to raise `radius`
(1.6 blocks) or the sample count if the contact shadows need to read stronger.

## Limits

- Screen-space AO cannot see geometry that is off screen or behind the camera, so a
  wall just outside the frame casts no contact shadow into the frame edge.
- Applying it to the whole colour multiplies direct sunlight too, which is not
  physically right - AO should only attenuate ambient. Splitting the ambient out in
  the resolve would need the ambient term isolated there; the 0.85 strength is the
  compromise.
- The blur is a 4x4 box, so very thin geometry (a single block) gets its shadow spread
  a little wider than it should be.
